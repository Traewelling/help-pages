title: "🩹 Sticker"
---

We have printed stickers for the community.
As long as the stock lasts, we can send you some by mail.

Please send us an e-mail to [sticker@traewelling.de](mailto:sticker@traewelling.de) with your address and a digital
stamp for a standard letter.
We cover our [costs](../../governance/finance/index.md) from voluntary support, and unfortunately we cannot pay for stamps out of
that.

You can buy a digital stamp in the Deutsche Post / DHL app, for example.
It starts with "#PORTO" followed by a code.

By default we put 10 stickers in the envelope.
If you need more or fewer, just tell us in your email, but please do not go overboard.
For larger amounts, please keep an eye on the postage: at some point a standard letter gets too heavy and needs a stamp
for a Kompaktbrief, the next size up.

We can send the letters anywhere where Deutsche Post delivers letters.
For shipments abroad, we can not guarantee.
In this case, please make sure that you use sufficient postage.
