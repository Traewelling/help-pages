---
title: "Checking in"
---

To record your journeys using Träwelling, follow these steps:
On the dashboard, enter your starting point into the "station" search field - whether it's a train station, bus stop, or underground station. You can then select your exact departure station from the drop-down menu. Träwelling also supports searching via RIL100 codes.

A list of all services departing from the selected stop in the near future will then appear. If you aren't travelling right away, you can change the date and time of your journey in the top left. In the top right, you can filter by specific modes of transport, helping you keep track of things at large metropolitan stations.
Once you've found your connection, click on it and select your destination stop in the next step.
If you like, you can now also add a status message to your check-in. Once everything looks good, click Check in! at the bottom and you'll have successfully created your first check-in on Träwelling - congratulations!

Those are the basics. However, Träwelling also lets you configure your check-in in more detail, depending on your needs.

Before you finally check in, you can:
- set the visibility of your status (Who is allowed to see this check-in?)
- specify the purpose of your journey (Are you travelling privately or commuting to work?)
- link an event (more about [events](events/index.md) )
- add friends or colleagues who are travelling with you
- add so-called tags. Tags are small pieces of additional information, such as your travel class, the vehicle number, or your role during the journey. You can also create your own tags.
